# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but own your impact.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, abusive comments, doxxing, and sustained disruption
are not welcome in this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or
contributions that violate this code of conduct.
